# Recorded sound effects

Every file here was cut from a CC0 (public domain) recording on [Freesound](https://freesound.org):
no attribution required, credited anyway. The sources are the 128 kbps "HQ preview" MP3s, cut,
faded and peak-normalised to -1 dBFS, then saved as 16-bit mono 44.1 kHz WAV. The recipes that
play them are in `../sounds.json` (layers of type `sample`, docs/AUDIO.md).
